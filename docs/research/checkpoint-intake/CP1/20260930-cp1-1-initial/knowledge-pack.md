## Native requirements derived from study

1. Separate requested structured-output mode, declared provider support and actual response validation.
2. Keep measured usage, estimates with method/version, and NOT_MEASURED distinct. Unknown price cannot become zero or satisfy a hard financial budget.
3. Bind immutable technical and persona profiles to each invocation.
4. Apply deadlines and cancellation through body/stream consumption, not just receipt of headers.
5. Reject silent schema/tool/provider downgrades and fallback to unavailable providers.
6. Sanitize typed failures; preserve evidence correlation and READ_ONLY authority.
7. An empty caller tools list is insufficient when a donor harness adds built-ins. Use native contracts, not the broad donor harness.
